## Why now
v1.1 is live but has ~single-digit daily visitors and zero ratings — the app works,
nobody's found it. Distribution is the lever. Two speeds:
- **Soft start (any time):** warm, personal channels — a friendly priest, the *chlapi*
  group you're already in. Low risk, real feedback, first ratings.
- **Louder push (recommended right after v1.2 ships):** the public v1.2 brings the nicer
  name **"Kam na mši"**, church photos, and the intro guide — a stronger first impression
  and better App Store discovery. Time the media/community posts for that moment.
