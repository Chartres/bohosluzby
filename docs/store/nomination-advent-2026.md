# App Store featuring nomination — Advent and Christmas 2026

For Pavol to submit. Not submitted by an agent. Apple asks for nominations ahead of time: two to
three weeks is the usual advice, and three months for a launch. Advent starts on Sun 29 Nov, so
submit **by Thu 12 Nov**.

**Before submitting:** version 1.5 with Czech listed as a language (the build after 91, see
`docs/review/2026-11-07-store-1.5.json`) should be submitted for review, so the nominated app is
the one Apple sees as Czech.

## Where (App Store Connect)
Your role needs to be Account Holder, Admin, App Manager or Marketing.

1. App Store Connect → **Apps** → **Kam na mši**.
2. **Distribution** tab → in the sidebar under **Featuring**, **Nominations**.
3. **＋** next to Nominations → **Create Nomination**.
4. Fill in the fields below. The nomination is saved as a draft until you press **Submit**.

## Fields (English, for Apple's form)

**Nomination type:** New Content

**Name:** Advent and Christmas Mass times across Czechia

**Description:**
> Kam na mši ("Where to Mass") shows the nearest Catholic Mass from where you are, with the time
> left to get there, for almost 4,000 churches in the Czech Republic. The schedules come from the
> Czech Bishops' Conference registry.
>
> For Advent and Christmas 2026 the app covers the services people look for once a year:
> roráty (the Czech Advent dawn Masses, often by candlelight) and the Christmas Masses. Times the
> registry lacks are added from the parishes' own Advent and Christmas notices, each with its
> source. Many Czechs who rarely go to church look for a Mass at Christmas; the app answers
> "which Mass can I still make tonight, and where" in one screen, offline if needed.
>
> Version 1.5 adds: tap a Mass time to set a reminder, add it to the calendar or invite someone;
> map groups that show the time of the Mass matching your choice; and Czech as the app's listed
> language. Free, no ads, no account; location stays on the phone.

**Publish start date:** 29 November 2026 (1st Sunday of Advent)

**Publish end date (if asked):** 6 January 2027 (Epiphany)

**Countries or regions:** Czechia (Slovakia as well, if offered: Slovak speakers use the Czech UI)

**Related app:** Kam na mši (id 6792305024)

**Supplemental materials (optional):** the 1.5 screenshots in `store-assets/ios/`; the web
pages at https://bohosluzby.dravec.org/advent/ once they are live on 28 Nov.

## What not to claim
- The roráty and Christmas town pages are on the web (`/advent/`), not inside the app. The app
  shows the same rows in its lists and on church pages. The description says only what the app does.
- Coverage depends on the pass (22–28 Nov). Don't name a number of towns before it is done.
